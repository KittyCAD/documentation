interface and sketching, then continue through solid creation, body modifications, Boolean
operations, transforms, construction geometry, and patterns.

The player below contains the complete series. Use its playlist menu or previous and next controls
to move between lessons without leaving the docs, or
[watch the full playlist on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLInwIcwpMOys).

## Lessons

1. **UI Overview** introduces the main areas of the Design Studio
   [workspace](/docs/zoo-design-studio/features/workspace).
2. **Sketch Basics** shows how to start a
   [sketch](/docs/zoo-design-studio/features/3d-design/parametric-modeling/sketching) and create the
   profiles used by downstream features.
3. **2D to 3D: Extrude, Sweep, Loft & Revolve** covers the primary
   [solid-creation tools](/docs/zoo-design-studio/features/3d-design/parametric-modeling/solid-modeling/create-solids).
4. **3D Modifications: Shell & Hole** demonstrates two ways to modify an existing
   [solid body](/docs/zoo-design-studio/features/3d-design/parametric-modeling/solid-modeling/modify-solids).
5. **3D Modifications: Fillet & Chamfer** covers edge finishing in
   [Modify Solids](/docs/zoo-design-studio/features/3d-design/parametric-modeling/solid-modeling/modify-solids).
6. **Boolean Operations: Union, Subtract & Intersect** shows how to
   [combine and cut bodies](/docs/zoo-design-studio/features/3d-design/parametric-modeling/solid-modeling/boolean-operations).
7. **Transformations: Translate, Rotate, Scale, Clone & Mirror** covers the main
   [transform tools](/docs/zoo-design-studio/features/3d-design/parametric-modeling/transforms).
8. **Construction Geometry: Offset Plane & Helix** introduces
   [construction references](/docs/zoo-design-studio/features/3d-design/parametric-modeling/construction-geometry).
9. **Patterns: Linear & Circular Pattern** demonstrates the
   [pattern tools](/docs/zoo-design-studio/features/3d-design/parametric-modeling/transforms/patterns).

Each lesson is also embedded on the corresponding reference page, where you can find detailed
instructions for the tools shown in the video.
