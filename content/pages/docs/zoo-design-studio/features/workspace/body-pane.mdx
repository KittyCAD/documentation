---
title: Bodies in the Feature Tree
excerpt: Select and control the visibility of current bodies from the Feature Tree in Zoo Design Studio.

---

Bodies appear directly in the [Feature Tree](/docs/zoo-design-studio/features/workspace/feature-tree).
Expand a body to see the sketches, regions, creation features, and later operations that contribute
to it. In an assembly, expand an imported part to find its bodies. Nested subassemblies retain their
own branches, and bodies created in the assembly remain at the assembly level.

## What You Can Do

- Select a body by clicking its row. The selection stays synchronized with the Scene and the
  associated KCL.
- Hide or show a body with the eye control on its row.
- Right-click a body for **Center on body**, **Hide body** or **Show body**, and **Delete body**.
- Right-click an already selected body without clearing the other selected bodies. Menu actions
  target the clicked body; **Center on body** selects it and centers the view on it. Show hidden
  bodies before centering them.
- Expand or collapse a body with its disclosure arrow. This does not change geometry visibility.
- Select individual pattern instances in the Scene; the source body row controls the source body.

## Control Body Visibility

Hover over, select, or keyboard-focus a body row to reveal its eye control. Click the control to hide
the body, then click it again to show the body. Hidden bodies retain a visible eye control and a
muted label.

Visibility changes update the model's KCL with a `hide()` operation. A hidden body remains part of
the design and can still be restored from the tree; hiding it does not delete its geometry.

## Delete a Body

Right-click a body row and choose **Delete body**. This immediately adds a Delete feature to the model
history and removes that body from the Scene and the Feature Tree. Undo the operation, or remove the
Delete feature, to restore the body.

See [Delete](/docs/zoo-design-studio/features/3d-design/parametric-modeling/transforms/delete) for deleting multiple bodies and the differences between deleting, hiding, and deleting a feature.

## How the List Is Organized

Bodies are labeled `Body 1`, `Body 2`, and so on in display order. Consumed inputs and their
features appear in the resulting body's history. A shared sketch can appear beneath more than one
body; each entry refers to the same feature.

3D patterns, nested patterns, and edits to their instances stay in the source body's history.
Pattern instances do not add body rows. Select individual instances in the Scene when needed.

Imported parts, bodies, and feature groups start collapsed. Expansion choices are retained while
editing a sketch, rebuilding the model, or closing and reopening a parent branch. Choices are scoped
to the current file.

## What You Cannot Do

- Rename or reorder bodies in the tree.
- Use a body row to select individual faces or edges. Select those in the Scene.
- Treat visibility as deletion. Hidden bodies remain in the model and in its KCL.

## Key Differences in This Area

- One scrolling tree connects current bodies with their contributing operations.
- Selecting a body works at the geometry level while remaining linked to its source code.
- Visibility is parametric model state recorded in KCL, not a temporary viewport-only setting.
