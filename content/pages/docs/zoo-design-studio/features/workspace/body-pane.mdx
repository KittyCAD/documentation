The Bodies section appears beneath the
[Feature Tree](/docs/zoo-design-studio/features/workspace/feature-tree) in the Scene browser tray beside the 3D scene.
The existing Bodies layout pane also remains available.
It lists the 3D bodies that currently result from your model, with one row for each body.
The sections collapse independently: click the Bodies heading to hide or reveal its list.
Click Scene browser or press `Shift+T` to collapse or expand the whole tray.

Use the Feature Tree to understand how the model was built. Use the Bodies section when you want to
work with the resulting geometry without finding the operation that created it.

## What You Can Do

- Select a body by clicking its row. The selection stays synchronized with the Scene and the
  associated KCL.
- Hide or show a body with the eye control on its row.
- Right-click a body and choose **Delete** to remove it from the model.
- Select and control individual copies created by a pattern. Each pattern instance has its own row.
- Resize the Scene browser tray by dragging its right edge. Feature Tree and Bodies share the same
  width and scroll area. The tray fits its contents rather than blocking unused scene space.

The list uses the same keyboard navigation as the Feature Tree. Focus it to navigate with the
arrow keys or type the start of a body label. The editable row-action shortcuts also apply:
`F2` enters actions, `Escape` returns to the row, and `Shift+F10` opens the context menu.

## Control Body Visibility

Click a body row's eye control to hide the body, then click it
again to show the body.

Visibility changes update the model's KCL with a `hide()` operation. A hidden body remains part of
the design and can still be restored from the section; hiding it does not delete its geometry.

## Delete a Body

Right-click a body row and choose **Delete**. This immediately adds a Remove feature to the model
history and removes that body from the Scene and the Bodies pane. Undo the operation, or remove the
Remove feature, to restore the body.

See [Delete](/docs/zoo-design-studio/features/3d-design/parametric-modeling/transforms/delete) for deleting multiple bodies and the differences between deleting, hiding, and deleting a feature.

## How the List Is Organized

The pane shows current model results rather than modeling history. Bodies are labeled `Body 1`,
`Body 2`, and so on in display order. When an operation consumes an earlier body to produce a new
result, the section focuses on the resulting body instead of keeping the intermediate body as a
separate entry.

Patterned geometry is expanded into separate entries for the source body and its copies, making it
possible to select or hide one instance at a time.

## What You Cannot Do

- Rename, reorder, or group bodies in the section.
- Use the section to inspect sketches, construction planes, faces, edges, or individual modeling
  operations. Use the Feature Tree for those items.
- Treat visibility as deletion. Hidden bodies remain in the model and in its KCL.

## Key Differences in This Area

- The Feature Tree shows the ordered operations that build the model; the Bodies section shows the
  current body-level results.
- Selecting a body works at the geometry level while remaining linked to its source code.
- Visibility is parametric model state recorded in KCL, not a temporary viewport-only setting.
