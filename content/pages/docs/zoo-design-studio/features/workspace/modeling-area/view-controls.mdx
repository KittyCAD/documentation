View Controls are the orientation and camera controls used to inspect the model from different
directions while working in the Modeling Area.

## What You Can Do

- Reorient the view while inspecting parts and assemblies
- Use orientation controls and gizmos to understand model direction
- Switch focus between different viewing angles while preparing selections and edits
- Combine camera movement with [Scene](/docs/zoo-design-studio/features/workspace/modeling-area/scene) interaction for point-and-click workflows

## Why They Matter

- They make it easier to understand model orientation before selecting geometry
- They help you inspect features from standard and angled views
- They reduce selection mistakes when working on complex geometry

## Gizmo Types

Zoo Design Studio supports more than one orientation gizmo style in the Modeling Area. The selected
style changes how the view-direction control looks, but not the underlying camera behavior.

### Axis Gizmo

The `Axis` gizmo shows the model orientation as labeled axes. This style is compact and works well
when you want a lightweight directional reference while keeping the scene unobstructed.

## Find Your Model

When using the local renderer, select **Recenter** from the view settings menu next to
the orientation gizmo to bring the model back into view. The action stays available
whether or not the model is visible. It keeps the current viewing direction and camera
projection, and fits the model itself, excluding reference planes, with a short ease-out animation.

If your model is mostly outside the view or too small to see, a one-time tip opens
the view settings menu and highlights **Recenter** after you stop navigating. This also
helps when zooming through a hole leaves no model visible. Dismiss the tip with **Got it**,
Escape, or a click outside the menu. The tip is remembered locally; using Recenter
before the tip appears also marks it as seen.

The action's arrow points toward an offscreen model when its direction is clear.
Inward arrows suggest zooming in; outward arrows suggest zooming out. A neutral
target icon is used when the hint is ambiguous.

You can also run **Zoom to fit** from the Command Bar at any time.

## Related Settings

Some view behavior is configured from user settings, especially:

- camera projection
- camera orbit
- gizmo type
- mouse controls

See [User Settings > Modeling](/docs/zoo-design-studio/features/workspace/settings/user-settings/modeling) for those preferences.
