We want you to be able to use Zoo Design Studio in a way that feels familiar. If you already use
another CAD tool, you have likely built up habits for moving around a model. Camera controls let you
choose familiar mouse gestures so you can concentrate on your design.

Open **Settings**, select the **User** tab, then open **Modeling**. The camera settings are user
preferences that apply across your projects. They change how you navigate and view your model;
the model's geometry stays the same.

## Choose your mouse controls

**Mouse Controls** chooses the buttons and keyboard modifiers used to navigate the view:

- **Pan** moves the view across the model.
- **Zoom** moves closer to or farther from the model.
- **Rotate** turns the view around the model so you can inspect it from another angle.

**Zoo** is the default scheme. Choose **Onshape**, **SolidWorks**, **NX**, **Creo**, **AutoCAD**, or
**CATIA** if its navigation gestures are familiar, or choose **Trackpad Friendly** to use keyboard
modifiers with a left-button drag. Selecting a scheme shows its pan, zoom, and rotate instructions
below the setting.

Notes:

- `Option` means `Alt` on Windows and Linux, and `Option` on macOS.
- `Cmd` means `Command` on macOS, `Win` on Windows, or `Super` on Linux.
- `Middle click` is usually the mouse wheel button.

## CATIA button transitions

CATIA uses the order in which you press and release mouse buttons:

1. Hold Middle and drag to pan.
2. While keeping Middle held, press and hold Left or Right to rotate.
3. Release Left or Right while keeping Middle held, then drag up or down to zoom.
4. Release Middle to finish. Press Middle again to start panning.

A quick Left or Right click while Middle stays held also switches to zoom; there is no need to
rotate first. You can also use the scroll wheel to zoom when no mouse buttons are held.

## Choose how rotation feels

**Camera Orbit** controls how the view turns when you use your scheme's rotate gesture:

- **Spherical**, the default, turns the view with reference to the model's up direction. Horizontal
  drags turn around that direction, and vertical drags tilt the view.
- **Trackball** allows free rotation, including rolling the view. Try it if you prefer to turn the
  model as though it were inside a ball.

The mouse scheme chooses the gesture that starts rotation; Camera Orbit chooses how that rotation
behaves. For example, you can use CATIA button gestures with either orbit setting.

## Choose the projection

**Camera Projection** controls how depth appears in the view:

- **Orthographic**, the default, keeps parallel lines parallel and shows objects at the same scale
  regardless of their distance from the camera. It is useful for comparing shapes and alignment.
- **Perspective** makes objects farther from the camera appear smaller, giving a sense of depth.

Projection and mouse controls are separate settings. You can change projection while keeping your
preferred navigation gestures.

## Navigate with touch

**Enable Touch Controls** is on by default. On a touchscreen, drag with one finger to orbit, drag
with two fingers to pan, and pinch to zoom. Touch gestures work separately from the selected mouse
scheme. **Trackpad Friendly** is a mouse scheme for trackpad clicks and keyboard modifiers; the
Enable Touch Controls setting controls touchscreen gestures.
